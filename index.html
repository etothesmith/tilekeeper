How to play

  • ↕Press and drag anywhere to run (Kip keeps running while you hold), use the joystick, or tap a tile to run there.
  • ◎Stand in a cube's path and press the brass button (or tap Kip) to mark the tile under him. Running with one thumb? Tap anywhere with a second finger. Then step off.
  • ✦When a cube rolls onto your mark, press the brass button again (or tap the cube) to capture it.
  • !Don't get rolled over, and don't let cubes fall off the edge. Too many spills and the floor crumbles.
